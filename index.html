Yes. The codebase uses modern Next.js 15 standards with clean, self-descriptive modular architecture. We include a comprehensive step-by-step setup guide covering Meta Business Manager verification, token generation, and database provisioning so you're never left guessing.

You are purchasing lifetime access to the complete SwiftFlow.ai SaaS platform source code, Supabase database schemas, automated appointment reminder cron scripts, the super admin console, and the complete high-ticket client pitch deck with onboarding SOPs.

Absolutely. If you operate an agency, ecommerce store, clinic, coaching program, or consultancy, you can deploy SwiftFlow.ai immediately for your own operations to automate lead qualification, team inbox management, and appointment bookings.
