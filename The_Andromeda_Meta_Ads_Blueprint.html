1.1 The Andromeda Hardware & Algorithmic Revolution

For nearly a decade, performance marketing on Facebook and Instagram operated on a deterministic model. Media buyers treated Ads Manager like an engineering console: they stacked 35 granular interest groups, layered 1% Lookalike Audiences derived from custom email lists, segmented campaigns into rigid demographic silos (e.g., "Men, 24–35, Mumbai, Interested in Entrepreneurship"), and manually forced the delivery engine to display ads to pre-selected buckets of human beings.

In 2026, that entire architecture is obsolete.

Meta executed what is known internally as the Andromeda Architecture—a total overhaul of its core ad retrieval and auction infrastructure powered by custom hardware-accelerated silicon: the Meta Training and Inference Accelerator (MTIA v2).

Prior to Andromeda, Meta's ad auction operated in discrete stages: candidate generation through coarse indexing, followed by heuristic filtering, and finally a separate lightweight neural ranking pass. Under Andromeda, this pipeline has collapsed into a unified, continuous deep learning retrieval and generative matchmaking engine.

Pillar 1: Advertiser Bid (The Wallet)

The bid represents the financial capital you are willing to deploy to secure the conversion. In digital product campaigns, 90% of advertisers operate on Highest Volume (auto-bidding), allowing Meta’s algorithm to bid dynamically to maximize conversions within the given daily budget.

However, when scaling aggressively (scaling beyond ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 per day), advertisers utilize Cost Caps or Bid Caps. A Cost Cap tells Andromeda: "Only enter auctions where you estimate you can acquire a customer at or below ₹499." If your creative lacks high EAR or high Ad Quality, your Cost Cap campaign will simply refuse to spend budget, protecting your capital from wasteful burn.

Pillar 2: Estimated Action Rate (EAR - The Mathematical Probability)

EAR is Andromeda’s predictive calculation: "What is the probability (0.00\% to 100.00\%) that user U_i will complete event E_{Purchase} upon viewing creative C_j?"

If you are selling a ₹2,999 Meta Ads Masterclass, and Andromeda displays your ad to an Indian college graduate who has never purchased an online course and possesses zero digital payment history, the EAR is near 0.05\%. To win that auction, your bid would have to be extraordinarily high.

Conversely, if Andromeda identifies a freelance performance marketer who has spent 45 minutes this week browsing digital marketing subreddits, engaged with marketing podcasts, and actively uses UPI for software subscriptions, the EAR may be 18.5\%. In this scenario, you win the auction at a fraction of the competitor's cost per mille (CPM) because Meta prioritizes ad delivery that guarantees a high transaction completion probability.

Pillar 3: Ad Quality / User Value (The Algorithmic VIP Pass)

Meta’s overarching corporate objective is user session retention. If users encounter intrusive, deceptive, low-quality, or abrasive advertisements, their app dwell time decreases, directly eroding Meta's long-term enterprise value. Consequently, Andromeda penalizes poor user experiences with extreme severity.

What Destroys Ad Quality in the Indian Market:

1. Engagement Baiting: Explicitly begging for comments, tags, or shares (e.g., "Comment 'COURSE' to get the link!" without meaningful value triggers algorithmic down-ranking; use native lead mechanisms or automated direct messaging integrations instead).

2. Sensationalism & Withholding (Clickbait): Using manipulative headlines like "You won't believe what happened next..." or deceptive claims.

3. Negative Feedback Velocity: Users clicking "Hide ad," "Report ad as misleading," or bouncing from your landing page within 3 seconds.

4. Landing Page Misalignment: Promising a free asset in the creative and redirecting users to an aggressive, unannounced paywall.

What Maximizes Ad Quality:

1. Positive Dwell Time: Users stopping their rapid scroll to watch the first 15 seconds of a video or reading through a well-crafted static carousel.

2. Meaningful Organic Interaction: High-intent comments debating the concepts introduced in the video, tagging colleagues for educational value, or saving the post.

3. Low Bounce Rates & High Landing Page Dwell Time: Users clicking through and spending 90+ seconds reading your sales letter or watching your VSL.

When an ad achieves both high EAR and high Ad Quality, Meta’s auction discount mechanism activates. You can out-compete multi-crore enterprise competitors even with a smaller daily budget because your Total Value score dominates the auction.

1.5 The Entity ID & The Creative Similarity Penalty

One of the most catastrophic mistakes made by media buyers transitioning to the Andromeda era is the Creative Similarity Trap.

Under older workflows, advertisers would take a winning video ad, change only the first three words of the primary text, export the video with a slightly different background color, and launch five separate ad sets claiming they were "testing five new creatives."

In Andromeda, this triggers the Creative Similarity Penalty.

When you upload an ad asset, Andromeda’s perceptual hashing and multi-modal embedding models assign the creative an Entity ID.
